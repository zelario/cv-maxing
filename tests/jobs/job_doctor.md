# General Practice Physician / Primary Care

**Company / Location:** SaúdeViva Clinic Network · Coimbra / On-site

**Role Summary:** Outpatient primary care, chronic disease management, clinical examinations, and preventive medicine in a modern private medical clinic.

## Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct medical consultations, physical examinations, and comprehensive patient history taking.
- Prescribe evidence-based diagnostic tests and medical pharmacotherapy.
- Execute triage and early intervention for acute outpatient clinical presentations.
- Maintain accurate, structured electronic health records.

## Mandatory Requirements (Must-Have):
- Integrated Master's degree (or equivalent MD qualification) in Medicine.
- Active license / registration with the Medical Association with general practice autonomy.
- Up-to-date certification or demonstrated proficiency in Basic Life Support (BLS).
- Strong compassionate communication and interpersonal patient-care skills.

## Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have):
- Advanced Life Support (ALS) certification.
- Prior clinical rotation experience in emergency departments or outpatient health clinics.
- Familiarity with clinical software suites.
