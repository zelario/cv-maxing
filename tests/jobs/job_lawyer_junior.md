# Junior Associate Lawyer (Corporate & Commercial Law)

**Miranda, Bastos & Associados · Lisbon / Hybrid**

Miranda, Bastos & Associados is a premier Portuguese business law firm providing specialized legal counsel to multinational corporations, institutional investors, and innovative high-growth enterprises. ### About the Role

We are seeking a high-performing **Junior Associate Lawyer (Advogado/a Júnior)** to join our Corporate and Commercial Law practice group in Lisbon.

* Draft, review, and negotiate a wide array of commercial agreements, including service agreements, distribution contracts, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), supply terms, and partnership covenants.

* Conduct in-depth legal research across Portuguese and European Union legislation, case law (*jurisprudência*), and legal doctrine to draft legal opinions and memoranda.
* Liaise with public regulatory bodies, the Commercial Registry (*Conservatórias do Registo Comercial*), and tax authorities regarding corporate filings.
* Assist in pre-litigation commercial dispute resolution, settlement negotiations, and legal risk assessments.
