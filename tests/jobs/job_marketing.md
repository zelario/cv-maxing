# Growth Marketing & Paid Media Specialist

**Company / Location:** Elevate Commerce Solutions · Lisbon / Remote

**Role Summary:** Overseeing and scaling paid acquisition channels across e-commerce and lead-generation verticals, optimizing ROAS and customer lifetime value.

## Key Responsibilities:
- Plan, deploy, and optimize paid acquisition campaigns across Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max) and Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram).
- Execute structured A/B tests on ad creatives, copy angles, and landing pages to lift conversion rates (CRO).
- Configure and maintain tracking events, user funnels, and attribution in Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
- Generate weekly executive reporting on core KPIs (CAC, LTV, ROAS, CTR, Blended CPA).

## Mandatory Requirements (Must-Have):
- Minimum 2 years of documented experience directly managing performance ad budgets.
- Deep technical fluency inside Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads.
- Strong quantitative capability with Google Analytics 4.
- Understanding of sales funnel stages and conversion copywriting principles.

## Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have):
- Active Google or Meta professional certifications.
- Working familiarity with email lifecycle automation tools (e.g., Klaviyo, HubSpot).
- Experience within high-growth digital agencies or fast-paced startups.
