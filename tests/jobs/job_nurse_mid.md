# Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Staff Nurse

**Central University Hospital · Porto / On-site (Rotational Shifts)**

### About Central University Hospital

Central University Hospital is a major tertiary academic medical institution recognized for excellence in specialized medical care, emergency response, and intensive trauma intervention. Our Intensive Care Department is equipped with state-of-the-art monitoring technology, providing multidisciplinary life support to critically ill patients across the northern region.

### About the Role

We are recruiting a dedicated **Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Staff Nurse (Enfermeiro/a de Cuidados Intensivos)** to join our Adult Critical Care and Resuscitation Department.

In this high-acuity environment, you will deliver specialized nursing care to adult patients experiencing single- or multi-organ failure, requiring continuous hemodynamic surveillance, invasive mechanical ventilation, and vasoactive pharmacology titration.

### What You'll Do

* Perform continuous invasive and non-invasive hemodynamic monitoring (arterial lines, central venous pressure, cardiac output, telemetry).
* Administer, titrate, and closely calculate continuous infusions of critical medications, including vasoactive amines, sedatives, analgesics, and neuromuscular blockers.
* Manage, monitor, and assist in airway care for intubated patients receiving invasive and non-invasive mechanical ventilation.
* Execute specialized nursing interventions including endotracheal suctioning, arterial blood gas sampling, wound dressing, and urinary/nasogastric catheterization.
* Rapidly detect acute patient deterioration, initiate emergency stabilization, and perform advanced cardiopulmonary resuscitation protocols.
* Enforce rigorous infection prevention and hospital-acquired infection control measures in strict compliance with ICU protocols.
* Maintain precise, real-time clinical documentation of vital parameters, fluid balances, and interventions in the hospital's critical care information system.
* Provide compassionate, structured communication and emotional support to families of critically ill patients.

### What We're Looking For

* Bachelor's degree (Licenciatura) in **Nursing** from an accredited nursing institution.
* **Active professional practicing license with the Nursing Council (Cédula Profissional da Ordem dos Enfermeiros)**.
* Proven clinical work experience or certified post-graduate clinical training in **Intensive Care (ICU), Intermediate Care, Emergency, or Resuscitation**.
* Practical mastery of clinical pharmacokinetics, critical medication dosage calculation, and emergency drug administration.
* Demonstrated competency in advanced resuscitation techniques and emergency patient stabilization.
* High emotional resilience, acute situational awareness, and the ability to make rapid clinical decisions under pressure.
* Strong commitment to ethical nursing practice and multidisciplinary teamwork.

### Nice to Have

* 1 to 2+ years of prior bedside experience in an adult polyvalent intensive care unit.
* Training in continuous renal replacement therapies (CRRT) or extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO).
