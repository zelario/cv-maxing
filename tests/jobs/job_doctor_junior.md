# General Practice Physician (Primary Care)

**SaúdeViva Healthcare Network · Coimbra / On-site**

### About SaúdeViva Healthcare Network

SaúdeViva is a leading Portuguese private healthcare network committed to delivering accessible, compassionate, and high-quality clinical care. Our outpatient medical centers across central Portugal provide integrated clinical services, preventive medicine, chronic pathology follow-up, and family health consultations, serving over 150,000 registered patients.

### About the Role

We are looking for a dedicated **General Practice Physician (Médico de Clínica Geral / Medicina Geral e Familiar)** to join our clinical team at the Coimbra Medical Center.

You will provide comprehensive primary medical care, conduct patient examinations, diagnose and manage acute and chronic conditions, perform clinical triage, and collaborate within a multidisciplinary clinical team comprising nurses, medical specialists, and diagnostic technicians.

### What You'll Do

* Conduct outpatient medical consultations, thorough medical history taking (anamnesis), and detailed physical examinations for adult and pediatric patients.
* Diagnose acute clinical presentations, formulate evidence-based treatment plans, and prescribe appropriate pharmacological therapy.
* Order, interpret, and evaluate complementary diagnostic tests (laboratory analyses, electrocardiograms, imaging studies).
* Provide proactive preventive healthcare, routine checkups, immunization guidance, and lifestyle counseling.
* Manage and monitor adult patients with chronic medical conditions (e.g., hypertension, diabetes mellitus, dyslipidemia).
* Perform clinical triage and early stabilization for urgent medical presentations before emergency referral if required.
* Maintain complete, accurate, and confidential electronic health records (EHR) in compliance with clinical protocols and patient privacy standards.

### What We're Looking For

* Integrated Master's Degree (Mestrado Integrado) or equivalent university degree in **Medicine**.
* **Active medical registration and valid license with the Medical Association (Cédula Profissional da Ordem dos Médicos)** with general practice autonomy (*autonomia profissional / internato geral concluído*).
* Valid, up-to-date certification in **Basic Life Support (BLS / SBV)**.
* Sound clinical acumen, diagnostic reasoning, and strict adherence to evidence-based medical guidelines.
* Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills, demonstrating empathy, active listening, and patient-centered care.
* Ability to work effectively within an interdisciplinary medical environment.
* Fluency in Portuguese with professional working proficiency in English.

### Nice to Have

* Current certification in **Advanced Life Support (ALS / SAV)**.
* Prior clinical rotation or residency experience in hospital emergency departments or primary healthcare centers (*Centros de Saúde*).
* Familiarity with clinical record software systems commonly used in Portugal (e.g., SClínico, SAM, or private clinical EHRs).
* Participation in clinical research, medical conferences, or accredited continuing medical education (CME).
