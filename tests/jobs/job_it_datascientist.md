# Data Scientist Intern

**Company / Location:** Revolut · London / Hybrid

**Role Summary:** Internship role working within cross-functional teams to develop statistical models, conduct large-scale dataset analysis, and design A/B experiments.

## Key Responsibilities:
- Analyze complex datasets to uncover user behavioral trends and business growth opportunities.
- Build and evaluate predictive statistical and machine learning models.
- Design, execute, and analyze product A/B tests and experimentation.
- Communicate quantitative findings through clear visual dashboards, reports, and presentations.

## Mandatory Requirements (Must-Have):
- Enrolled in or recently graduated with a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or Engineering.
- Solid foundations in statistics, probability, and quantitative analysis.
- Hands-on experience with Python and SQL.
- Working familiarity with standard data libraries (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn).
- Fundamental understanding of Machine Learning and model validation techniques.

## Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have):
- Practical exposure to A/B testing or causal inference.
- Experience with data pipelines or distributed cloud tools.
- Personal, academic, or open-source projects showcasing practical data science application.
