# Junior Associate Lawyer / Corporate & Commercial Law

**Company / Location:** Miranda, Bastos & Associados Law Firm · Lisbon / Hybrid

**Role Summary:** Legal advisory services for domestic and international corporate clients in commercial agreements, corporate governance, and regulatory compliance.

## Key Responsibilities:
- Draft, review, and negotiate commercial contracts (service agreements, distribution, NDAs, supply terms).
- Assist in corporate actions: drafting shareholder resolutions, incorporations, and statutory amendments.
- Support corporate due diligence audits and compile findings memoranda.
- Conduct legal research across case law, statutes, and civil/commercial doctrine.

## Mandatory Requirements (Must-Have):
- Degree in Law (LL.B.); Master's in Corporate or Forensic Law strongly valued.
- Active registration with the Bar Association (or final internship phase towards qualification).
- Deep understanding of Commercial Company Law and Civil Law.
- Rigorous drafting precision and legal analytical reasoning.
- Fluent professional and legal English proficiency (written and spoken).

## Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have):
- Familiarity with GDPR and data privacy compliance in commercial operations.
- Practical experience with electronic court and commercial registry platforms.
