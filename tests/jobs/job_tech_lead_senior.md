# Senior / Staff Tech Lead (Distributed Systems)

**Aether Cloud Platforms · Lisbon / Porto / Remote**

### About Aether Cloud Platforms

Aether Cloud Platforms develops next-generation, high-performance infrastructure and data streaming platforms for enterprise clients across Europe and North America. We build mission-critical, fault-tolerant distributed systems capable of processing millions of operations per second with sub-millisecond reliability.

### About the Role

We are seeking an experienced **Staff / Tech Lead** to drive the technical direction, architectural soundness, and engineering excellence of our core distributed platform squad.

In this role, you will be the technical authority for key architectural decisions, collaborating closely with product management, engineering directors, and individual engineers to translate complex business challenges into resilient, scalable systems. You will balance hands-on design and critical path implementation with high-impact technical mentorship.

### What You'll Do

* Lead the architectural design and evolution of high-throughput, low-latency distributed microservices and event-driven data streaming pipelines.
* Drive technical standards, architectural RFC processes, code quality initiatives, and system resilience practices across engineering squads.
* Author clear architectural documentation, trade-off analyses, and technical roadmaps aligned with organizational goals.
* Mentor senior and mid-level software engineers through system design reviews, pair programming, and architectural feedback.
* Troubleshoot complex distributed systems anomalies, performance bottlenecks, and concurrency issues in production.
* Work with infrastructure/DevOps engineers to ensure clean cloud-native deployments (AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform) and robust observability.

### What We're Looking For

* **7+ years** of professional experience in software engineering, with proven leadership as a **Tech Lead, Staff Engineer, or Principal Engineer**.
* Deep expertise in designing and maintaining **distributed systems, microservices architectures, and event-driven architectures** (e.g., Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ).
* Strong programming proficiency in modern backend languages suited for distributed computing (such as **Go, Python, Java, or Rust**).
* Solid foundation in relational and non-relational database design, query optimization, and transaction boundaries (**PostgreSQL, Redis**).
* Proven track record of mentoring software engineers and driving architectural alignment without direct hierarchical authority.
* Demonstrated experience with cloud environments (**AWS or GCP**), container orchestration (**Docker, Kubernetes**), and Infrastructure as Code (**Terraform**).
* Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical trade-offs to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

### Nice to Have

* Degree in Computer Science, Informatics Engineering, or related technical field.
* Experience with distributed tracing and observability tools (OpenTelemetry, Datadog, Prometheus).
* Familiarity with Domain-Driven Design (DDD) principles and CQRS patterns.

### What We Offer

* Competitive compensation package and equity options.
* Full flexibility for remote, hybrid, or office-based work in Lisbon or Porto.
* High degree of technical autonomy and opportunity to shape the platform architecture.
