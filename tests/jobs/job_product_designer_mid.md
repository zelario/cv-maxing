# Product Designer (UI/UX) - Web & SaaS

**Vektor Studio · Porto / Remote**

### About Vektor Studio

Vektor Studio designs and scales digital products for global tech startups, B2B SaaS platforms, and innovative consumer brands. We pride ourselves on creating elegant, accessible, and intuitive user experiences that turn complex domain workflows into delight for daily users.

### About the Role

We are looking for a collaborative, user-centric **Product Designer (UI/UX)** to lead the design lifecycle of web applications, client portals, and SaaS dashboards.

You will take ownership of end-to-end product design: from understanding user needs through qualitative research and discovery to crafting high-fidelity design specifications and scalable Design Systems in Figma. You will collaborate daily with Product Managers, UX Researchers, and Frontend Engineers to ship polished, meaningful features.

### What You'll Do

* Design intuitive user flows, information architectures, wireframes, and high-fidelity interactive prototypes in **Figma**.
* Lead user discovery sessions, qualitative interviews, usability testing, and heuristic evaluations to validate design hypotheses.
* Build, maintain, and expand our cross-platform **Design System** (component libraries, auto-layout variants, variables, design tokens).
* Partner closely with frontend developers to ensure pixel-perfect implementation, responsive fidelity, and smooth design-to-development handoffs.
* Advocate for accessibility standards (**WCAG 2.1 AA**) across visual hierarchy, contrast ratios, and interactive states.
* Present design rationale, user insights, and design iterations clearly to internal teams and executive stakeholders.

### What We're Looking For

* **2+ years** of hands-on experience in Product Design or UI/UX Design, specifically focused on **web applications or SaaS platforms**.
* Exceptional proficiency in **Figma** (mastery of auto-layout, nested component variants, variables, and interactive prototyping).
* Proven track record in creating and maintaining **Design Systems** and reusable component libraries.
* Strong grounding in UX research methodologies: usability testing, user journey mapping, and heuristic reviews.
* A strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design thinking, problem framing, iteration processes, and polished visual design.
* Understanding of modern web capabilities and constraints (responsive grids, flexbox behavior, component state management).
* Fluent written and verbal communication in English and Portuguese.

### Nice to Have

* Experience with design-to-code inspection tools (Storybook, Figma Code Connect).
* Familiarity with micro-interactions and motion design (Principle, After Effects, or Lottie).
* Knowledge of product analytics tools (Mixpanel, Hotjar, PostHog) to inform design decisions.

### What We Offer

* Flexible remote-first work culture with team gathering hubs in Porto.
* Modern hardware budget (MacBook Pro, 4K display, ergonomics).
* Annual education budget for conferences, design courses, and books.
* Collaborative and transparent work environment where design is a core strategic pillar.
