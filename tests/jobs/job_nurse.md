# Intensive Care Unit Staff Nurse / ICU

**Company / Location:** Central University Hospital · Porto / On-site (Rotational Shifts)

**Role Summary:** Specialized critical care nursing for adult patients requiring vital function support, advanced hemodynamics, and continuous monitoring.

## Key Responsibilities:
- Invasive and non-invasive hemodynamic monitoring of critically ill ICU patients.
- Titration and continuous infusion of high-alert emergency medications and vasoactive drugs.
- Care management for intubated patients receiving invasive mechanical ventilation.
- Rigorous execution of hospital-acquired infection prevention protocols.

## Mandatory Requirements (Must-Have):
- Bachelor's degree (B.Sc.) in Nursing.
- Active practicing registration with the National Nursing Council.
- Verifiable clinical training or work experience in Intensive Care (ICU), High Dependency, or Advanced Emergency Care.
- Competence in advanced resuscitation and emergency patient management.

## Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have):
- Specialist Nurse title in Medical-Surgical Nursing.
- Official ALS (Advanced Life Support) certification.
- 1 to 2+ years of dedicated ICU experience.
