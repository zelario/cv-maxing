## Form Title
**Resume Evaluation Study: Human vs. Artificial Intelligence Tailoring**

### Form Description:
> Thank you for participating in this evaluation study conducted for the **CV Maxing** project (Department of Informatics Engineering, University of Coimbra).  
> The objective is to evaluate the authenticity, naturalness, and quality of resumes tailored to job postings across different methodologies.
>
> **How this works:**
> - You will review **3 candidate scenarios** in software engineering and technology.
> - For each scenario, you will see a **job description summary** and **3 alternative resume versions** (Version A, Version B, Version C), all rendered with identical professional visual styling.
> - In each scenario, one version was tailored manually by a human engineer/recruiter (**Human**), one was produced by our evidence-grounded multi-agent assistant (**CV Maxing**), and one was produced by an unconstrained commercial AI (**Baseline LLM / ChatGPT**).
> - Version order has been randomized across scenarios.
>
